I am a dwarf that was adopted by a human family at an early age. 

They named me Hilda because they were unfamiliar with the dwarven race, had read somewhere that dwarven girls had beards, so they assumed I was a baby girl. They have learned more since then.

Neither my parents nor I know where I come from, or why I was left at their doorstep.
Like my parents, I serve Lunar, the goddess of knowledge and magic. 
She is the patron goddess of our town, and other deities are rarely discussed. 
Not because they're racist or anything, just because they don't tend to pop up in conversations. 
The times that they did, I wasn't really paying attention (and I'm kinda stupid anyways), so I never realised that other deities exist.

Although I have been raised helping around in the temple, I only started my religious education in September T-1. 
The third semester of education, which started in September T+0, Lunar's students will typically spend the semester on a mission, which consists of a long journey focused on collecting knowledge. 
Some choose to spend this semester in a populated area learning about a new culture, whereas others will choose to live as hermits and spend the semester documenting forest plant life.
I was on my way to study Westwall, when I happened to get abducted by bandits. Oops.
