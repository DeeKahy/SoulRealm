**Founded five years ago** by Liliana Mornstar and bankroled by [[Xera]], an **unbound succubus** who saw opportunity in the mortal realm's entertainment industry. After centuries in the infernal planes, Liliana managed to kill her summoner, making her a rare "unbound" entity with autonomy to pursue her own interests.

## Establishment Details

**Siren's Embrace operates as a high-end entertainment venue** in [[Azurvale]] the city's upscale district. While officially registered as a "specialized hospitality club," locals know it offers unique experiences unavailable elsewhere. The establishment **maintains perfect legal compliance**. **All taxes are paid promptly**, and health inspectors find nothing amiss during their scheduled or unscheduled visits.

## Staff and Services

The club employs **twelve succubi under binding contracts** that Liliana personally crafted. These contracts include strict clauses preventing harm to clients while allowing the succubi to harvest *"nutrients"* during *encounters*. Each succubus signed these contracts in exchange for sanctuary in the mortal realm and protection from infernal authorities.

The **shapeshifting abilities** of the staff represent the club's main attraction. Clients can experience encounters with virtually any form they desire, from idealized versions of celebrities to fantastical beings—all within ethical boundaries established by Liliana. This service has made Siren's Embrace legendary among those who can afford its steep membership fees.

## Reputation and Clientele

The establishment maintains a **high class reputation**. The ladies of the night are well known to be shape shifters, and most clients go there to have intimate encounters with famous people or other personal matters.

## The Manager

Liliana presents herself as a **mysterious but charismatic businesswoman** with unusual connections. She maintains a public persona as an eccentric entrepreneur with a gift for understanding desires. Only her inner circle knows her true nature, though rumors constantly circulate about her seemingly ageless appearance and uncanny insights into people's secret wishes.

Despite her demonic origins, Liliana has developed a genuine appreciation for mortal society and has no desire to disrupt it—her business is simply too profitable to jeopardize with unnecessary chaos.
