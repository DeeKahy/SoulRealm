# Goroth
Goroth is the [god](https://ellc.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") of the earth and patron of craftsmen. His Taboo is the turning of lesser materials into expensive materials such as gold, mithral, or gemstones. Boxxy describes this as "_Turning unshiny things into shiny things._"


# Lunar
Lunar is the [Godess](https://ellc.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") of magic, and is the closest god to [Twitchy](https://ellc.fandom.com/wiki/The_God_of_Chaos) in terms of friendlyness.


Her Divine Item is the LIAR

Divine item: **The Librarium Infinitus Arcana Refactorium** or **LIAR** - A divine item in the form of a book, that forms a face out of letters when activated. Can give absolutely any information in exchange for a price related to how hard it is to get without it. The price is related to the desired information and gaining information causes the divine item to become dormant for a day afterwards.


# Diwata

**Diwata** is a god of nature and is often worshiped by [dryads](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Dryad "Dryad"). She is distantly related to [Apido](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Apito "Apito"). Known to changes genders but is normally referred to by female pronouns.

She normally appears as a squirrel with antlers that's covered in moss and leaves but she is capable of shapeshifting. She uses her shapeshifting to sleep with different creatures, she then gives birth to regular versions of those creatures.

Known for doing strange things such as impregnating herself then eating her own offsprings and forming cities with her poop.


Her natural form is a giant squirrel-like creature with antlers that's covered in moss and leaves.
