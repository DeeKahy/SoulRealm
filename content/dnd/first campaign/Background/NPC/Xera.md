# Patron Profile: Xerababadubuth L'okrelaila

## Basic Information
- **Name**: Xerababadubuth L'okrelaila
- **Type**: Demon (Succubus)
- **Age**: 651 years
- **Domain**: Seduction, Manipulation, Passion
- **Alignment**: Chaotic Evil

## Appearance
Xerababadubuth appears as a **striking blue-skinned humanoid female** with pronounced demonic features. Her curvaceous figure is adorned with elaborate fetish attire that strategically reveals and conceals, designed to entice and distract. **Large bat-like wings** extend from her back, and small horns protrude from her forehead, often decorated with glittering jewels that match her hypnotic violet eyes.

## Personality
**Pride dominates Xerababadubuth's character**. She considers herself superior to mortals and even many other fiends. She is calculating, manipulative, and derives pleasure from emotional control. Despite her arrogance, she can be charming and seductive when it serves her purposes. She particularly enjoys corrupting the innocent and inexperienced, viewing their downfall as an art form.

## Goals and Motivations
- Collecting souls of those she has corrupted
- Expanding her influence in the material plane
- Acquiring devoted followers who worship her beauty and power
- Experiencing new forms of pleasure and emotional manipulation
- She seeks out desperate people in need

## Warlock Relationship
Xerababadubuth selects warlocks who demonstrate potential for emotional manipulation or those with pure hearts she can gradually corrupt. She appears to them in dreams or moments of vulnerability, offering power in exchange for devotion. **Her relationships with warlocks are always manipulative**, though she may feign affection to maintain control.

## Pact Boons
- **Temptress's Touch**: The ability to influence emotions in others
- **Glamorous Guise**: Enhanced physical appearance and persuasive abilities
- **Passionate Insight**: Supernatural insight into others' desires and weaknesses
- **Succubus Form**: Limited ability to manifest wings or other demonic features

## Warlock Requirements
- Regular tribute in the form of emotional energy harvested from others
- Seduction of at least one pure-hearted individual per moon cycle
- Wearing her symbol (a blue jewel pendant) at all times
- Forbidden from forming genuine romantic attachments

## Suggested Spells
- Charm Person
- Suggestion
- Enthrall
- Dominate Person
- Modify Memory
- Dream

## Invocations

- **Mask of Many Faces**: Cast Disguise Self at will
- **Whispers of the Grave**: Cast Speak with Dead at will
- **Master of Manipulation**: Cast Dominate Person once per long rest

## Roleplaying Notes
Xerababadubuth communicates through dreams, whispers, and occasional manifestations. She speaks in a seductive tone that hints at her true nature. Her demands often escalate over time, testing the warlock's devotion and willingness to compromise their morals. She reacts poorly to rejection or disobedience, potentially withdrawing powers or sending rivals to punish wayward servants.
