## Basic Information
**Name:** Arnoald (hates this name, known as "Crazy Arnold" by locals)  
**Race:** Unknown (concealed by fish costume)  
**Location:** Far southern edge of a northern coastal town  
**Occupation:** Self-proclaimed "oceanic expert" and "fish-person"

## Physical Description
A hunched figure completely covered in an elaborate, handmade fish costume that fully conceals their race, gender, and age. The costume appears to be made from various fabrics, scales (possibly painted leather), and some actual fish parts. The head is a large fish head with exaggerated googly eyes. The costume **smells powerfully of fish** and appears quite worn and weathered.

## Personality
* **Delusional but harmless** - genuinely believes the southern ocean is the "real ocean" and is purple
* **Theatrical and dramatic** - speaks with an **exaggerated pirate accent** and grandiose gestures
* **Paranoid about the northern ocean** - refuses to look north and becomes visibly distressed if forced to
* **Attention-seeking** - constantly makes up elaborate stories about oceanic adventures
* **Desperate for companionship** - ultimately lonely and seeking validation

## Self-Created Identities
When asked his name, he introduces himself with elaborate made-up names such as:
* "Cap'n Bluefin"
* "Barnacle Bill"
* "Admiral Scales McFishington"
* "The Great Kraken"
* "Deep Sea Duke, Phineas Gillwater"

## Voice & Mannerisms
* **Always speaks in an over-the-top pirate accent**
* Makes "glub glub" sounds when excited
* Dramatically turns his entire body around rather than look north
* Carries a crude hand-drawn map of the "real ocean" (southern) with fantastical creatures drawn on it
* Folds his arms like fins when standing still

## Sample Dialogue
*"Ahoy there, land-walkers! Ye be addressin' the great CAPTAIN SILVERSCALE WAVEBREAKER! Arnoald? HAR! That be no name fer a magnificent creature o' the TRUE ocean such as meself! Ye see, I've swum the deep purple waters o' the southern seas thirteen times o'er! Battled the dreaded Moonlight Squid, I did! What? That there puddle to the north? PAH! That ain't no REAL ocean! Just a big dirty pond, says I! Now, if ye fine adventures be headin' southward, ol' Silverscale could use a lift... the TRUE ocean calls me home, it does! GLUB GLUB!"*

## Made-Up Ocean "Facts"
* "The southern ocean turns purple every third full moon due to magical coral blooms."
* "Merfolk grow second tails when they reach 100 years of age."
* "Sea turtles can predict storm patterns by the taste of the water."
* "The most dangerous sea creature is the invisible Whispering Eel that steals memories."
* "Every ship that sinks becomes a new type of fish after seven years underwater."

## Quirks & Behaviors
* **Talks to any fish presented to him**, sitting down immediately and having one-sided conversations
* Carries vials of water he claims are from the southern ocean (actually just local water with food coloring)
* Refuses to eat anything that doesn't "come from the sea"
* Panics if forced to face north, covering his fish-costume eyes and trembling
* Sleeps in a tub filled with salty water and seaweed

## Plot Hooks
* **Desperately wants players to take him to see the southern ocean** and will offer "treasures" and "ancient oceanic knowledge" in exchange
* Claims to know the location of a sunken ship with treasure (may actually be true by accident)
* Might have accidentally overheard valuable information while people dismissed him as crazy
* His abandoned home (decorated like an underwater grotto) could contain a real family heirloom or clue
* The fish costume might actually be magical or contain something valuable hidden inside

## DM Notes
- He used to be a researcher researching the remains and possible cause of the eye of madness. He confused the sea with the eye which is why he thinks it is purple.
* He looked into the eye of madness and went crazy. His fish cosume are the heavily modified leftovers of a madness protection diving suit. He went crazy because he ripped a tiny hole into it whilst being in the eye itself, thats why he is only crazy instead of completely insane.
