## Maze Hamster (Surprise Enemy)

**Stats:**
- **AC:** 8
- **HP:** 1
- **Speed:** 15 ft.
- **Attack:** +0 to hit, 1 piercing damage (tiny bite)
- **Perception DC:** 10

**Encounter Setup:**
When players are exploring the maze, have them roll a Perception check (DC 10). If they **fail**, the hamster gets a surprise attack that automatically hits for 1 damage. If they **succeed**, they notice the tiny creature and can instantly defeat it without rolling initiative.

**Description:** *"A small ball of brown fur darts from a crack in the wall. It's a seemingly ordinary hamster, though its eyes gleam with unusual intelligence as it attempts to nip at your ankle."*
