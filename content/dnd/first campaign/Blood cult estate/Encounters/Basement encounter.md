Here the players encounter Landemere Vane Who is part of the blood cult and whos mother is the "master"

## Setting the Scene
> *"As you descend into the basement, the temperature drops noticeably. Magical lights flicker along the stone walls, illuminating a circular chamber filled with arcane equipment. In the center stands a disheveled man in tattered robes, frantically scribbling notes at a desk covered in scrolls. He looks up suddenly, his eyes widening in shock and anger."*
>
> *"YOU!" he shrieks, pointing a trembling finger. "You're the ones who interfered with my teleportation matrix! My calculations were perfect until YOUR magical signatures appeared from nowhere! Do you have ANY idea what you've cost me?!"*

## Tactics
- **Master:** Stays at range, uses Magic Missile first, then Ray of Frost on the strongest-looking character
- **Snake:** Appears when Master falls, emerges from his robes hissing angrily
- **Snake Tactics:** Slithers along walls and ceiling to attack from unexpected angles

## Room Features
- **Arcane Circle:** Glowing runes on floor that flash when stepped on (harmless)
- **Reagent Shelf:** Can be toppled (DC 10 Strength) to create difficult terrain
- **Bubbling Cauldron:** Can be knocked over (DC 12 Strength) to create a hazard (1d4 acid damage)
- **Magical Crystals:** Glow brighter when spells are cast nearby

## Dialogue Prompts
- **When defeated:** *"No! Not yet! My research... Azmos, protect me!"* (snake emerges)
- **Snake's appearance:** *The Master's body trembles as a glowing emerald snake slithers from his sleeve, growing to six feet long as arcane energy courses through its scales.*

## Possible Outcomes

### When Players Win:
> *"With a final hiss, the snake convulses and dissolves into sparkles of arcane energy. The basement falls silent except for the gentle bubbling of the cauldron. Among the Mages possessions, you find a pouch of coins and an unusual item that seems to pulse with lingering magic."*

## Rewards
- **Gold:** 75 gold pieces in a velvet pouch
- **Magical Item:** **Amulet of Chaos Magic** - Once per day, wearer can cast a random cantrip (roll 1d4):
  1. Light (creates light on an object for 1 hour)
  2. Mending (repairs a small break or tear)
  3. Minor Illusion (creates a small sound or image)
  4. Prestidigitation (small magical trick)
- **Bonus:** The Master's research notes, which explain that his teleportation spell accidentally pulled the players from their world. The notes hint at a way to return home (hook for future adventures)

## Experience Points LEVEL UP
Award 100 XP to the party for completing this challenging encounter.
