## Adventure Overview

**Concept:** Players wake in a strange hedge maze, surrounded by unusual hamsters and isolated in a magical oasis within a vast desert. They must navigate the maze, defeat the Hamster King, and explore the mansion to gather supplies for their desert journey home.

**Structure:**
1. Awakening in the maze
2. Maze exploration (with hamster encounters)
3. Hamster King boss fight
4. Mansion discovery and exploration
5. Cannibal encounter
6. Revelation of the desert surroundings
7. Preparation for departure

## Part 1: The Awakening

### Opening Scene

Read to players:
> *"You all wake up on soft grass. Everyone has a throbbing headache and no memory of how you got here. Looking around, you see tall green hedges forming what appears to be a maze. The air is pleasantly warm, and birds chirp somewhere in the distance. Introduce yourselves to each other."*

**DM Notes:**  (encourrage them to be sisters)
- Give players time to introduce their characters
- If they ask what they have with them: Each has basic clothing and perhaps a simple weapon (dagger, staff) but no supplies
- When ready to proceed: *"As you orient yourselves, you notice narrow pathways stretching in multiple directions. The hedge walls tower above you at 8 feet tall. Which way do you wish to explore?"*

## Part 2: Maze Exploration

### Random Encounters (Roll 1d6 at each intersection)
1. **[[Surprise Hamster Attack]]** - Use the Maze Hamster stats provided
2. **Strange Scratching Sounds** from within the hedge (nothing happens, just building tension)
3. **Small pile of seeds** and nuts (harmless, can be collected)
4. **Torn piece of clothing** caught on a branch (hint of previous visitors)
5. **Dead end** with scratched markings on the ground
6. **Hamster tracks** leading in a specific direction (toward the Hamster King)

### Maze Navigation
- The maze has **6 intersections** before finding the Hamster King encounter. If they do they can leave or fight.
- If they leave they go straight to the mansion

## Part 3: Hamster King Encounter
> *"The maze path opens into a circular clearing. In the center sits a small mound topped with a miniature throne made of twigs and cloth scraps. Atop it sits a chubby golden-furred hamster wearing a bottle cap crown adorned with colorful beads. It clutches a toothpick scepter wrapped in shiny foil. Three smaller brown hamsters stand guard in formation, their tiny eyes fixed on you. The crowned hamster squeaks commandingly and raises its tiny scepter as the minions bare their teeth."*

## Part 4: The Mansion

### Discovery
> *"Beyond the next turn in the maze, the hedges open up to reveal a grand mansion. Three stories tall with elegant architecture, it stands in stark contrast to the maze. The front doors are partially open, and the windows reflect the bright sunlight."*

### Exterior Features
- Marble stairs leading to the entrance
- Overgrown garden surrounding the mansion
- Small fountain (not working) near the entrance
- High windows on upper floors

## Part 6: Exploration and Supplies

### Key Rooms
1. **Kitchen** - Dried food, water flasks, cooking supplies
2. **Library** - Books about desert survival, maps showing the mansion as an oasis.
3. **Bedrooms** - Clothing, backpacks, personal items
4. **Basement** - Locked door (DC 5 to pick) containing the [[Basement encounter]].

### The Discovery
When players look out windows from upper floors or explore the perimeter of the estate:
> *"Beyond the lush grounds surrounding the mansion and maze, you see nothing but endless sand dunes stretching to the horizon. The verdant area around you appears to be a perfect circle of life in an otherwise barren desert. At the boundary, you can see the greenery abruptly stopping, as if cut by an invisible line."*

## DM Tips

### Running the Game
- **Keep it simple:** Focus on describing the immediate surroundings
- **Yes, and...:** Encourage player creativity and build on their ideas
- **Rule of cool:** When in doubt, let players do the awesome thing they want to try
- **Track time:** Mention the sun's position changing to create urgency

### Combat Tips
- **Initiative:** Roll a d20 + any dexterity bonus to determine turn order
- **Attacks:** Roll d20 + attack bonus vs AC to hit
- **Damage:** Roll specified dice for damage
- **Positioning:** Use coins or tokens to show where everyone is

### Adventure Conclusion
Players can:
- Prepare and leave for the desert (follow-up adventure)
- Stay in the mansion temporarily
- Search for the mysterious "Master" mentioned by the cannibals
- Find clues about how they arrived in this strange place

**Quick Reference: Key Stats**

- **Maze Hamster:** AC 8, HP 1, Attack +0 (1 damage)
- **Hamster King:** AC 12, HP 8, Scepter +2 (1d4 damage)
- **Hamster Minions:** AC 9, HP 2, Attack +1 (1d2 damage)
- **Cannibals:** AC 12, HP 10, Knife +2 (1d4+1 damage)
