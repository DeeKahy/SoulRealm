# Quick Reference: Random Player Actions

## Environment Interactions

### Burning Through Hedge Maze
- **DC 12 Intelligence (Nature) or Survival check** to successfully start a controlled fire
- **Success**: Creates 5-foot opening in 1d4 minutes
- **Failure by 5+**: Fire spreads uncontrollably; 1d4 fire damage to nearby players
- **Note**: Hedge is slightly damp (25% chance rain extinguishes fire)

### Climbing Hedge Walls
- **DC 10 Strength (Athletics)** check
- **Success**: Player can see 1d4 sections of maze ahead
- **Critical Failure (1)**: Fall into thorny section; 1 damage and clothes tear

## Hamster Interactions

### Offering Food to Hamsters
- **DC 8 Animal Handling**: Hamster becomes temporarily friendly
- **Natural 20**: Gain tiny hamster companion that can squeeze through small gaps
- **Natural 1**: Hamster bites finger (no damage) and alerts nearby hamsters

### Using Hamster as Scout
- **DC 12 Animal Handling**: Hamster follows simple direction before returning
- **DC 15**: Hamster brings back a tiny object (seed, button, etc.)

## Mansion Exploration

### Finding Secret Passages
- **DC 12 Investigation** reveals hidden servants' passages between rooms
- **25% chance** any bookshelf conceals a hidden door
- **Secret basement wine cellar** (DC 15 Perception) contains 1d4 expensive bottles

### Cannibal Psychology
- **DC 10 Deception**: Convince cannibals you're poisonous to eat
- **DC 12 Intimidation**: Scare off one cannibal
- **DC 14 Persuasion**: Get cannibals to reveal the Master's schedule

## Creative Solutions

### Using Water from Fountain
- **If purified** (spell or boiling): Safe to drink
- **Splashing on magical items**: 10% chance of revealing hidden properties
- **Pouring on overgrown garden**: 50% chance of awakening a friendly plant creature

### Makeshift Tools/Weapons
- **Breaking furniture**: Yields improvised clubs (1d4 damage)
- **Kitchen items**: Pots can be helmets (+1 AC), knives as daggers
- **Chandelier swinging**: DC 10 Acrobatics for dramatic combat entrance (advantage on next attack)

### Desert Survival Ideas
- **Creating sun shade**: Extends water supplies by 50%
- **Following stars**: DC 10 Survival reduces travel time by 25%
- **Digging for water**: 5% chance of finding underground spring
