



As players enter the kitchen, they're struck by a **jarring contrast between mundane domesticity and horrific reality**. The space features:

- A **massive stone hearth** dominating one wall, large enough to roast a full-sized creature, with **suspicious iron hooks** dangling above
- **Ancient butcher blocks** stained deep brown-black despite apparent attempts to scrub them clean
- **Walls lined with well-maintained copper cookware** hanging from racks, all oversized and gleaming with recent use
- An **ornate collection of specialized cutting implements** arranged with ritualistic precision
- A **heavy wooden preparation table** in the center with **leather restraints** attached to each corner
- **Herb bundles** hanging from ceiling beams, some recognizable cooking herbs mixed with **strange fungi and unknown plants**

Players searching the kitchen might discover:

- A **leather-bound recipe book** with dishes described in coded language and **ritualistic preparation instructions**
- **Hidden compartments** beneath floorboards containing **preserved "special" ingredients**
- A **locked pantry** requiring a key or lockpicking, containing **jars of preserved organs** labeled with dates and names
- **Detailed anatomical drawings** tucked between normal cookbook pages
- A **wine cellar trapdoor** leading to both conventional wines and bottles of **thick red liquid** arranged by "vintage"
- **Personal effects** from previous victims hidden in drawers
- **Ceremonial dining implements** with cult symbols engraved on the handles

The air carries a **sickly-sweet smell** beneath layers of herbs and spices, and occasional **suspicious stains** mar the otherwise meticulously maintained space.
