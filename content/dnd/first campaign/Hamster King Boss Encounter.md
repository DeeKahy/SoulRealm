
## Combat Stats

### Hamster King
- **AC:** 12
- **HP:** 8
- **Speed:** 20 ft.
- **Attacks:**
  - **Scepter Bonk:** +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., 1d4 damage
  - **Royal Command:** The King points its scepter at one minion, granting it advantage on its next attack

### Hamster Minions (3)
- **AC:** 9
- **HP:** 2 each
- **Speed:** 15 ft.
- **Attack:** +1 to hit, 1d2 piercing damage

## Tactics
- King stays at the rear, using Royal Command each turn
- Minions attempt to surround party members
- If King drops to 2 HP, it tries to flee into a hole in the hedge

## Room Features
- **Clearing:** 15 foot diameter circular area
- **Throne Mound:** Small dirt pile in center with twig throne
- **Escape Tunnels:** Small holes at base of hedges
- **Scattered Seeds:** Can be kicked at hamsters (DC 8 Dex) to distract for 1 turn
- **Pebbles/Twigs:** Difficult terrain for tiny creatures

## Possible Outcomes

### If Players Win:
> *"With a final squeak, the Hamster King falls. The remaining minions scatter in panic, disappearing into tiny holes in the hedges. The bottle cap crown tumbles from the King's head, revealing a tiny gemstone embedded in its inner rim."*

**Reward:** Bottle cap crown with gemstone (worth 5 gold)

### If King Escapes:
> *"The Hamster King squeaks in alarm as it scurries toward one of the holes in the hedge. Its minions throw themselves in your path, buying their monarch precious seconds to escape."*

### If Players Spare the King:
> *"Recognizing its defeat, the Hamster King bows its tiny head and chittering softly, removes its crown as tribute. It backs away slowly before turning to scurry into a hole in the hedge wall, its remaining minions following close behind."*

## Experience Points
Award 50 XP to the party for completing this encounter.
